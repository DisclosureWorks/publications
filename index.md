# DisclosureWorks Publications

Edition label: `2026-11`

**What DisclosureWorks Publications are.** Finished policy papers: formal memos and research papers written for the people who make decisions. Each opens with a bottom-line-up-front summary, carries footnotes, and comes as a Word and PDF download. Each paper is an output of the [Parallax](https://disclosureworks.ai/parallax/) research tool, not documentation of it: the DisclosureWorks Sourcebook holds the short reference pages, and a paper applies the same method to one question in depth. Parallax is a general research tool; these papers use two subjects to demonstrate it. The research paper, *National Security Power and Constitutional Imbalance*, examines how Congress's oversight of national security power has worked and failed. The legislation paper assesses the Epstein Files Transparency Act II (H.R. 9694 / S. 5005): what the bill gets right, where it fails, and fourteen specific fixes. The legislation paper assesses whether the bill would work as written, not whether it should pass. The Epstein record is the demonstration subject because its released documents are public and large; later editions will take up subjects unrelated to that case.

**The problem this solves.** Policy writing about government power and the investigations that follow it is usually built on commentary rather than primary documents, asserts without evidence the reader can check, and does not show how its conclusions were reached. DisclosureWorks Publications are written the other way round: from the record, with footnotes checked against sources.

**Who it is for.** Members of Congress and their staff working on the legislation, and the journalists, advocates and researchers following it.

**How it is made.** Each paper is built on primary sources, such as the bill text, the enacted law, court records and agency documents. Footnotes are checked against sources, and where we hold no copy the note says so. Papers are reviewed by independent AI reviewers before release, and the publisher approves each version by name. Each paper is published as a fixed, versioned edition with checksums, and the sources we hold are retained with their fingerprints.

**Where it is going.** The Sourcebook is updated every month with a new edition. Publications can be updated when there is new work. Corrections and feedback are welcome now at `corrections@disclosureworks.ai`.

**Get notified.** To hear about new editions, follow our [web feed](/feed.xml) or watch this site's [GitHub repository](https://github.com/DisclosureWorks/publications) for releases. No sign-up is needed.
