# Parallax Publications

Edition label: `2026-10`

**The problem this solves.** Policy writing about government power and the investigations that follow it is usually built on commentary rather than primary documents, asserts without evidence the reader can check, and does not show how its conclusions were reached. Parallax Publications are written the other way round: from the record, with every footnote checked against a source we hold.

**What Parallax Publications are.** Finished policy papers: formal memos and research papers written for the people who make decisions. Each opens with a bottom-line-up-front summary, is fully footnoted, and comes as a Word and PDF download. Each paper is an output of the Parallax research tool, not documentation of it: the Research Atlas holds the short reference pages, and a paper applies the same method to one question in depth. Parallax is a general research tool, not a project about one case; this first edition uses two subjects to demonstrate it. The research paper, *National Security Power and Constitutional Imbalance*, examines how Congress's oversight of national security power has worked and failed. The legislation paper assesses the Epstein Files Transparency Act II (H.R. 9694 / S. 5005): what the bill gets right, where it fails, and fourteen specific fixes. The legislation paper assesses whether the bill would work as written, not whether it should pass. The Epstein record is the demonstration subject because its released documents are public and large; later editions will take up subjects unrelated to that case.

**Who it is for.** Members of Congress and their staff working on the legislation, and the journalists, advocates and researchers following it.

**How it is made.** Each paper is built on primary sources, such as the bill text, the enacted law, court records and agency documents. Every footnote is checked against its source, each paper is reviewed by several independent AI reviewers before release, and the publisher approves each version by name. Each paper is published as a fixed, versioned edition with checksums, and the sources behind it are retained with their fingerprints.

**Where it is going.** Publications and the Research Atlas are updated every month with a new edition. Corrections and feedback are welcome now at `corrections@disclosureworks.ai`.
